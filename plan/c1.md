# C1: German retrieval for RAG on one workstation

Test design, written 2026-10-09 and committed before any C1 run. Shared
rules (machine record, bootstrap, sanity gate, licence class, exposure):
`plan/c0-common.md`. A change after the commit goes into a new dated
section at the end.

## Question

Which open embedding model finds the right German passage best, and at
what cost in time and memory, when a company indexes its own documents on
one RTX 4090? Does a local reranker on top of the best embedder add enough
to be worth its latency?

## Why it matters for a German SME

Almost every in-house AI project starts with search over the company's own
documents (RAG). Retrieval quality caps the answer quality of everything
built on top of it, and the embedder is the one component that has to
process the whole document stock, so its indexing speed decides whether a
nightly re-index is realistic.

| GermanDPR (secondary) | `mteb/GermanDPR`, split `test` | `64a4860e55ba6d8fcb923d5306d08e08b1c72794` (MTEB results were computed on the source `deepset/germandpr` at `5129d02422a6`) | CC BY 4.0 | 1,025 queries, 2,876 passages |

No sampling: every query of both sets is used. The texts are not committed
(MIRACL is ShareAlike and large); `cases/c1/` holds the dataset ids,
revisions and a SHA-256 over the loaded query, corpus and qrels rows.

Not used: `mteb/germanquad-retrieval` (CC BY 4.0, but 474 passages and
multilingual-e5-large already at nDCG@10 0.947, so it cannot separate good
models); GerDaLIR (no licence in the card); the MTEB German1, Healthcare
and Legal retrieval sets (not public).

Exposure: multilingual-e5 and bge-m3 list MIRACL train among their
fine-tuning data; the dev queries used here are not in train, but the
corpus is the same German Wikipedia. GermanDPR train may be in the training
data of any of the models. Both are public since 2021/2022.

## Method

Exact search (inner product over normalised vectors, `numpy` or
`faiss-cpu` flat index), no approximate index, so the number measures the
model and not an ANN setting. Ranking of the full corpus per query, top 100
kept.

## Metrics

- Primary: nDCG@10 on MIRACL de (pytrec_eval, MTEB's definition).
- Secondary: Recall@100 and MRR@10 on both sets; nDCG@10 on GermanDPR.
  On GermanDPR Recall@100 is near 1.0 for most models (e5-large: 1.0 in
  MTEB), so it is reported but not interpreted.
- Bootstrap unit: query.
- Machine: indexing throughput in passages per second (batch 64, fp16,
  timed over the whole corpus), query latency in ms per query at batch 1
  (encode plus search), peak VRAM during indexing, GPU energy for the whole
  index in joules and per 1,000 passages. Reranker: added ms per query for
  100 pairs.

## Baseline

BM25 above. skill = (nDCG@10 - BM25) / (1 - BM25).

## Sanity gate

Reference: MTEB results repository (`embeddings-benchmark/results`,
read 2026-10-09):

- `BAAI/bge-m3` at `5617a9f6`, MIRACLRetrievalHardNegatives dev `de`:
  nDCG@10 0.5759, Recall@100 0.9626.
- `intfloat/multilingual-e5-large` at `ab10c1a7`, GermanDPR test:
  nDCG@10 0.8290.

`bge-m3` runs first. Tolerance: nDCG@10 within 0.015 of 0.5759. Then
`e5-large` on GermanDPR within 0.015 of 0.8290 (our e5 revision differs
from the one MTEB used; a gap here is checked against MTEB's own revision
before anything else is concluded).

## Expected GPU time

About 1.5 h (indexing 71k passages per model is minutes for the small
models, about 25 min for Qwen3-Embedding-8B; rerankers 305 x 100 pairs).

## 2026-10-09: preparation notes (before any C1 run)

- The planned MIRACL revision `95c8db7d4a6e` no longer exists on the hub:
  the dataset history was rewritten on 2025-09-10 and the oldest commit
  left is from that day. The run uses the head of `main` on 2026-10-09,
  `332a9acb49f5e83d5397683f79d23e588f685916` (its last commit only adds an
  eval config). The counts match the design exactly: 305 queries, 71,277
  passages, 3,144 qrels (including qrels with score 0, which pytrec_eval
  treats as not relevant, as MTEB does). Hashes in `cases/c1/manifest.json`.
  If the sanity gate misses, the changed revision is the first suspect.
- Passages are encoded as `title + " " + text`, as MTEB does for corpora
  with titles.
- The query side runs at batch 1 only: the scored ranking is the one from
  the timed batch-1 pass (encode plus exact search per query), so the
  latency and the quality come from the same calls.
- BM25 uses no stopword list (the design names only the stemmer).
- `e5-large-instruct` and both Qwen3 embedders use the web search
  instruction from their cards: "Given a web search query, retrieve
  relevant passages that answer the query".
- Reranker jobs are templates until the embedder results exist
  (`bench/queue/jobs/c1/later/`), because the base embedder is chosen
  after the fact.

## 2026-10-10: after the run, notes on scoring

Written after all eight embedder arms had run (2026-10-09) and before the
rerankers ran. Nothing above is changed; these notes say how the plan was
applied where it left room, and where the run fell short of it.

- Gates: `bge-m3` 0.5767 on MIRACL de (reference 0.5759, tolerance 0.015)
  and `e5-large` 0.8284 on GermanDPR (reference 0.8290). Both passed.
- Scoring: `bench/score-cap.py` recomputes every metric from the per-query
  rows (pytrec_eval values written by the runner) and adds the intervals.
  The paired test against the best arm is a bootstrap of the per-query
  nDCG@10 difference over queries (same generator); p is twice the smaller
  share of resampled differences on either side of 0. `bench/check-cap.py`
  recomputes nDCG@10, Recall@100 and MRR@10 of four arms from the stored
  top 100 and the qrels with its own code. All agree within 0.001; the one
  gap at the fourth decimal (BM25 on GermanDPR, 0.5116 against 0.5111) comes
  from the order of tied BM25 scores.
- Peak VRAM: reported twice, over the whole job (queue status, the value in
  the capability map) and during the MIRACL index section, both as
  nvidia-smi memory.used minus the idle value before the model loaded.
  Qwen3-Embedding-8B peaks at 21.8 GB at batch 64; torch's own count of
  allocated memory agrees (21.9 GB), so this is use, not reserve. A smaller
  batch would need less; the plan fixes batch 64.
- Throughput: `plan/c0-common.md` asks for throughput numbers repeated three
  times. The runner indexed each corpus once per arm, so indexing
  throughput is a single measurement. Not repeated, since the order between
  arms (44 to 2,377 passages per second) is far larger than run-to-run
  noise.
- Rerankers: the best embedder by nDCG@10 on MIRACL de is `qwen3-emb-8b`
  (0.613), chosen after the fact as planned. Both reranker jobs were
  enqueued on 2026-10-10 with that base run
  (`bench/queue/jobs/c1/09-bge-rerank.json`, `10-qwen3-rerank.json`).
- Capability map: the rule of `plan/ROADMAP.md` (best by the primary
  metric, ties to the smaller peak VRAM) is applied by the scorer; no tie
  arises in C1, because the paired test separates `qwen3-emb-8b` from every
  other arm.
