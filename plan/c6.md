# C6: German reading comprehension (extractive QA)

Test design, written 2026-10-09 and committed before any C6 run. Shared
rules: `plan/c0-common.md`. A change after the commit goes into a new dated
section at the end.

## Question

Given a German passage and a question, does a local LLM answer with the
right words from the passage, and does it beat a small trained reader?

## Why it matters for a German SME

This is the answer step of RAG (C1 is the search step): the model has the
right passage and must pull out the fact without adding or inventing. A
model that answers outside the passage is a liability in contracts, manuals
and policies.

## Data

- GermanQuAD, `deepset/germanquad`, licence CC BY 4.0 (card, read
  2026-10-09), split `test`: 2,204 questions over German Wikipedia
  passages, three answers per question (76 removed by the authors). The
  original download (`germanquad.s3.amazonaws.com`) no longer exists
  (NoSuchBucket, 2026-10-09); the data comes from the Hugging Face parquet
  conversion, branch `refs/convert/parquet` at `a2f3a59f0be8`, file
  `plain_text/test/0000.parquet`, SHA-256
  `c154513e016e73e4e32b46b48010f24a435ad7b0e96cc3858cfc20c7d29649bc`
  (downloaded 2026-10-09). The main branch is at `fff05ceaf2ff`.
- LLM sample: 1,000 questions, drawn by sorting on `id` and taking the
  first 1,000 of a permutation from numpy `default_rng(20261009)`. The
  trained reader and the sanity check use all 2,204.
- Committed: `cases/c6/` with the 1,000 sampled rows (CC BY 4.0, attribution
  to deepset in the README).
- Exposure: German Wikipedia passages are in nearly every pretraining set;
  GermanQuAD itself is public since 2021.

LLM prompt, fixed and committed verbatim: German instruction to answer with
the shortest exact span copied from the passage, or `keine Antwort` if the
passage does not contain it (GermanQuAD test has no unanswerable
questions, so `keine Antwort` is always wrong and counted). Ollama, JSON
format `{"answer": string}`, temperature 0, seed 1, thinking off.

## Metrics

- Primary: F1 (SQuAD v1.1 official definition: lowercase, punctuation and
  English articles removed, max over the three answers), over the 1,000
  sampled questions.
- Secondary: exact match; span faithfulness (share of answers that occur
  verbatim in the passage); F1 with German articles (der, die, das, ein,
  eine) removed instead of English ones, as a sensitivity check.
- Bootstrap unit: question; paired bootstrap between arms.
- Machine: median ms per question, peak VRAM, GPU energy per 1,000
  questions.

## Baseline

First sentence of the passage that shares the most content words with the
question, returned whole (a lexical overlap baseline, committed before the
run).

## Sanity gate

Reference: GermanQuAD performance table in the card of
`deepset/gelectra-large-germanquad` (from Möller, Risch, Pietsch 2021,
read 2026-10-09): GELECTRA-large-GermanQuAD on the test set EM 68.6, F1
88.1. `gelectra-large` runs first on all 2,204 questions; F1 must lie
within 2 points of 88.1.

## Expected GPU time

About 2.5 h (1,000 questions x 6 LLMs at 1 to 3 s each, plus the reader on
2,204).
