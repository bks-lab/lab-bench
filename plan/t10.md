# T10: throughput with batching

Test design, written 2026-10-12 and committed before any T10 run. A change
after the commit goes into a new dated section at the end.

## Question

How many routing decisions per second does GLiNER Decide make on one
workstation when it is fed in batches instead of one question at a time,
and do the answers stay the same?

## Why it matters

Fast classification into given labels is GLiNER Decide's declared strength
(ROADMAP, home ground). route1 measured it at batch 1 (14 ms per question).
Bulk jobs (a mailbox backlog, a nightly ticket import) run batched, and the
sizing of an offer depends on that number.

## Data

route1, MASSIVE de: the 2,974 German requests, 5,948 questions, in file
order. No new data.

## Arm

`gliner2.5-multi-decide` (`fastino/GLiNER2.5-multi-Decide`, Apache-2.0, open
source), the multilingual Decide model of route1, mode prompt, exactly the
route1 call of `bench/run-gliner.py`, through gliner2's own batched scorer
(`Classifier.scorer.batch_score`, one compiled schema per question, which is
the path `Classifier.score` takes with batch size 1). Runner:
`bench/t10/run_t10.py`.

Local LLMs are not re-measured: C8 already measured route1 decisions per
hour for nine models at Ollama concurrency 1, 4 and 8 (`plan/c8.md`), and
the T10 findings cite those numbers next to GLiNER's.

## Method

- Batch sizes 1, 8, 32 and 128, three timed passes each over all 5,948
  questions (c0 rule for throughput: three repeats, mean and range). One
  untimed warm-up batch before every pass.
- Wall time per pass includes tokenisation and schema handling, with a CUDA
  synchronise at the end; model load is reported separately.
- The choices of the first pass of every batch size are stored.

## Metrics

- Primary: decisions per second per batch size (mean and range of three).
- Secondary: agreement of each batch size's choices with batch 1 (padding
  can move a near tie), peak allocated GPU memory per batch size, GPU
  energy per 1,000 decisions from the queue's sampler.

## Sanity gate

The batch 1 choices must agree with the committed route1 run 1 of the same
arm (`results/route1/gliner2.5-multi-decide/2026-10-09-run1.jsonl`, MASSIVE
de rows) on at least 99 % of the questions; otherwise the job exits 3 and
the throughput numbers are not reported.

## Expected GPU time

About 10 minutes (route1 batch 1: 5,948 x 14 ms = 83 s per pass; batched
passes are faster; 12 passes plus load).
